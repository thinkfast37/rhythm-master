Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 August 2026

Rhythm Master collects no personal data. There is no account to create, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

What stays on your device

The Patterns you create, your ratings and tags, and your settings are stored in the app's local storage on the device you use. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

In the store version, purchases, subscriptions and the free trial are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own privacy policies. The app asks the store which purchases are current in order to unlock itself; it does not receive your name, email address or payment details.

Submitting a Pattern

If you choose to submit a Pattern to the shared library, the app opens GitHub in your browser with the Pattern pre-filled. Anything you then post is under your own GitHub account and GitHub's privacy policy. The app itself never contacts GitHub.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.
