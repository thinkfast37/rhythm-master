The app runs entirely on your device. There are no accounts, and nothing you create is uploaded anywhere. Patterns you make are stored on the device you made them on.

Cancelling stops future renewals; it does not refund the current period.

Prices, billing, refunds and cancellation are handled by Apple or Google under their own terms. Rhythm Master never sees your payment details. Purchases can be restored on another device signed in to the same store account with Restore purchases.
